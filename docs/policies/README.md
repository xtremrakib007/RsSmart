## Before publishing
1. Replace every [PLACEHOLDER] with verified company details and contact information.
2. Have Malaysian counsel review applicability of the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), its amendments and current regulations, the Consumer Protection Act 1999 where applicable, Electronic Commerce Act 2006, tax/invoicing requirements, and sector-specific rules.
3. Confirm whether the business must register with the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and appoint a Data Protection Officer under current rules; assess applicability rather than assuming an exemption.
4. Document which entity is the data controller/data user and which role RsSmart performs for tenant customer data. Contract wording must match actual operations.
5. Do not claim that payment funds always settle directly to tenants until the chosen provider confirms this in writing and the integration is tested.
6. Make policy acceptance versioned and auditable. Show the applicable terms before purchase, record version/time/user, and provide users with a downloadable copy.
7. Publish the approved versions on the website and link them from signup, checkout, and app settings.
8. Make privacy rights, complaints, cancellation, refunds, support response times, and data deletion workflows operational before promising them.

Do not put secrets, access tokens, customer records, or production connection strings in these documents or GitHub.
