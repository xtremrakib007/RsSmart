## 1. Scope and instructions
Where RsSmart acts as processor, it processes personal data only to provide, secure, support and maintain the Service under documented customer instructions, unless law requires otherwise. Complete Schedule A with subject matter, duration, purposes, data categories and data subjects.

## 3. Safeguards and confidentiality
RsSmart will maintain appropriate safeguards as actually implemented, including access control, tenant isolation, protected secrets, encryption, logging, backups and incident response. Authorized personnel must be bound by confidentiality and access data only as needed.
